# Privacy policy

Galla is an app for running one shop. It has no server, no account of its
own, no ads and no analytics. The developer never receives any of your data.

## What stays on the phone

Products, stock, bills and shop details (name, address, phone number, UPI
ID) are kept in the app's own storage on the phone. The camera is used only
to read barcodes, and the barcode is read on the phone: no picture leaves it.

## When something leaves the phone

Only when you make it:

- **Sync between phones**, if you turn it on. Galla asks Google for access
  to Drive's hidden app folder (`drive.appdata`) in the Google account you
  pick, and keeps a copy of the shop's data there so the shop's other phones
  can read it. Galla cannot see any other file in that Drive, and no one else
  sees this folder. Google's own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  covers the sign-in and the storage.
- **Backups and slips** you save or share go where you send them.
- **Android's own backup**, if it is on in the phone's settings, can copy
  the app's data to the phone's Google account. Android does this, not
  Galla.

Galla turns off the usage logs that Google's barcode library (ML Kit) would
send, so they never leave the phone.

Galla's use of information received from Google APIs follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including its Limited Use requirements.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Galla deletes its data from the phone. To delete the synced
copy too, open [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com) as the shop's
account, then go to Settings → Manage apps → Galla → Delete hidden app data.
